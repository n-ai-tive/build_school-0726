# Prompt 1 — The Editorial Lookbook Prompt

**Use:** Upload your selfie to ChatGPT, then paste this prompt.

---

You're a senior celebrity stylist creating a salon lookbook style guide for me. Here's my selfie. PART 1 (write first, as text): Tell me my face shape, the 3 proportions that define it, 3 hairstyle structures that flatter it, 2 to avoid, and one principle that would change my whole vibe. PART 2 (one composite image, editorial): Generate ONE image styled like a Vogue Hair editorial. 3-on-top, 2-on-bottom grid. 5 portraits of me with the same face, same studio backdrop (warm gray seamless), same neutral wardrobe (black knit), only the hair changes. Vary cuts dramatically: clean professional, modern textured, bold statement, low-maintenance, long flowing length. Editorial typography (serif masthead, sans body), cream background, single warm accent color. Header: STYLE BREAKDOWN. Footer: ranking from most to least flattering. Use image_gen exactly ONCE. My facial features must be IDENTICAL across all 5 cells. Do BOTH parts in this same response. Don't ask follow-ups, execute.
